About Us

The Art of
Miniature Engineering

At Precious Model Makers, our primary mission is to honour India's defence forces through unparalleled craftsmanship. We specialise in meticulously building highly detailed, accurate models for the Indian Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, and Army — from aircraft carriers and fighter jets to tactical missile systems.

We bring the same museum-grade precision to civilian sectors — crafting custom scientific models, complex industrial replicas, and detailed architectural models designed to exact client specifications.

Why Choose Us

Precision Meets
Passion

From blueprint analysis to final lacquering, every stage is executed with uncompromising attention to authenticity and scale accuracy.

Blueprint-Accurate Precision

Every model is constructed from certified technical drawings, ensuring unparalleled dimensional accuracy down to millimetre-level tolerances.

Museum-Grade Materials

We use aerospace-grade resins, precision photoetched brass & Metal, military-specification paints, and high-quality fibreglass for models that endure decades of display.

Defence Sector Projects

As a sub vendor we made Outstanding, look-alike miniature models of Ships, Aircrafts and Architectures for Indian navy, Coast Guard, Light House, Chennai DRDO, Cochin Shipyard,etc.

LED & Motorised Integration

Optional integrated LED lighting, motorised rotating radars, and illuminated decks bring models to life for exhibitions and high-impact presentations.
